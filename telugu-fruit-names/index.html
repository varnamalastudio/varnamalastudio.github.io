How to practise (5 minutes a day)

  1. At snack time, hold up a fruit and say its Telugu name.
  2. Tap the sound here so your child hears the right sound.
  3. Ask "ఇది ఏమిటి?" (idi aemiti? — what is this?) and let them answer.
  4. Say the fruit names on the next video call.

Questions parents ask

What is banana in Telugu?

Banana is అరటిపండు (aratipandu).

What is mango in Telugu?

Mango is మామిడిపండు (maamidipandu).

What does పండు mean?

పండు (pandu) means fruit; పండ్లు (pandlu) is fruits.
