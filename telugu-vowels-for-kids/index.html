How to practise (5 minutes a day)

  1. Tap one letter, listen twice, then say it together.
  2. Tap its picture word and point to the picture.
  3. Trace the letter once on the printable page.
  4. Use the word on the next call with Ammamma or Tatayya.

Questions parents ask

How many vowels are there in Telugu?

Telugu has 16 vowels, called achulu (అచ్చులు). This page covers the first 10, from అ to ఏ.

What age can kids start learning Telugu letters?

Most children can start listening and tracing big letters from about age 4, a few minutes a day.

Is ౠ (roo) used in words?

Very rarely. Kids learn it as a sound in the alphabet; it has no common picture word.
