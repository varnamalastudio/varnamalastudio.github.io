How to practise (5 minutes a day)

  1. Pick one sentence for the week.
  2. Tap it and repeat it together, 5 minutes a day.
  3. Keep the printed call card next to the laptop or phone.
  4. Your child says it on Sunday’s call — then watch Ammamma’s face.

Questions parents ask

How do you say "Hello Grandma" in Telugu?

అమ్మమ్మా, నమస్తే! (ammammaa, namastae!) — Ammamma is mother’s mother; for father’s mother many families say నానమ్మ (naanamma).

How do you say "I love you" in Telugu?

నేను నిన్ను చాలా ప్రేమిస్తున్నాను (naenu ninnu chaalaa praemistunnaanu). At home, families often say నువ్వంటే నాకు చాలా ఇష్టం.

Why does my child reply in English?

English always works at school and home, so kids default to it. Ready sentences and a real reason to use them — like grandparent calls — help them switch.
